Embryo transfer due date calculator

For surrogates and intended parents after a frozen or fresh embryo transfer. Enter the transfer date and the embryo's age to see the due date and key milestones.

Embryo age at transfer

How it works: a pregnancy is dated from two weeks before conception. A day-5 embryo transfer therefore equals 2 weeks 5 days of pregnancy, so the due date is the transfer date plus 261 days (263 for day-3, 260 for day-6 embryos).
