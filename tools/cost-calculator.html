How we build these numbers

Each line is a range compiled from prices that agencies, clinics, law firms and non-profits published in 2026, rounded to the nearest US$500. US journeys commonly total about US$100,000-250,000; altruistic systems such as Canada and the UK are cheaper in fees but involve long waits; programmes in Mexico, Colombia and Georgia cost less but carry more legal risk.

Ranges are not quotes. Exchange rates, your clinic's success rates, and the number of transfers you need will move the total. Read our complete cost guide for the detail behind each line.
