Quick answers

Questions we hear every day

Is SurroMother a surrogacy agency?

No. SurroMother is an independent information and tools website. We do not recruit or pay surrogates, arrange surrogacy or hold funds. With your consent we can pass a consultation request to licensed professionals.

How much does surrogacy cost?

In the United States most journeys cost roughly US$100,000-250,000. Altruistic systems such as Canada and the UK cost less in fees but involve long waits. Use the cost calculator for an itemised estimate.

How long does surrogacy take?

Typically 15-24 months from first consultation to bringing a baby home, longer in altruistic countries where matching can take a year or more.

Who can become a gestational surrogate?

Most programmes look for people aged about 21-40 who have had at least one healthy pregnancy, are raising a child, have a BMI within clinic limits, do not smoke and live where surrogacy is lawful.

Where is surrogacy legal?

It varies widely. Most US states allow compensated surrogacy; Canada, the UK and Australia allow altruistic surrogacy only; many European countries prohibit it. See the law tracker for 48 countries.
