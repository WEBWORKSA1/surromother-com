Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy explains how SurroMother.com ("SurroMother", "we") handles personal information. Surrogacy is a sensitive subject, so we collect as little as possible and never sell personal information.

What we collect

  • Information you send us through forms (consultation requests, eligibility pre-screen results you choose to send, newsletter sign-ups, contact, pledges, contest entries, career and partner applications). This can include your name, email, phone, country, and the answers you give, which may include health-related information such as pregnancy history.
  • Technical data such as pages visited, the page you first arrived on, referrer and campaign tags, stored in your browser and attached to forms you submit so we can understand what helps.
  • Tool inputs (calculators, checklist, quiz) stay in your browser unless you choose to send them.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and provide the information you asked for.
  • If you tick the consent box on a consultation or pre-screen form, to share your request with up to three licensed professionals relevant to your situation and jurisdiction. We do not share it otherwise.
  • To send the newsletter if you subscribed (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To run contests, process pledges, review applications and improve the site.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

We rely on your consent (forms, newsletter, health-related answers you choose to send), our legitimate interests (site security and improvement) and contract (pledges, partnerships). You may withdraw consent at any time.

Form processing

Forms are delivered by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form-relay service. Messages are stored in our email account. We keep enquiries for up to 24 months, then delete them unless an ongoing relationship requires longer.

Cookies and advertising

We use a small amount of browser storage to remember your cookie choice, checklist progress and how you found us. We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info.
  • Choosing "Essential only" in our cookie notice asks Google to serve non-personalised ads.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may see Google's consent message, which records your choices.

Embedded YouTube videos load only when you click play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) domain.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, India's DPDP Act or California's CCPA), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to processing. Use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.

Children

SurroMother is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security and transfers

The site is served over HTTPS. Information may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States and Canada, with appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the date at the top.
