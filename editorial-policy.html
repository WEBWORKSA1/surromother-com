Editorial policy

How we decide what to publish, how we check it, and how we keep money away from the facts.

Sources

We prefer primary sources: statutes, regulators, court decisions and professional medical bodies. Where we rely on secondary sources such as law-firm explainers, reputable news outlets or published agency pricing, we say so and link to them. Every guide and country page lists its sources.

Review and updates

  • The law tracker is reviewed at least monthly and whenever a significant change is reported. Each page shows its review date.
  • Guides are reviewed at least every six months; costs are re-checked at least twice a year.
  • We are recruiting independent medical and legal reviewers; reviewer names will be shown on the pages they review.

What we won't publish

  • Invented testimonials, composite "real stories" or unverifiable success rates.
  • Content that encourages paid surrogacy where it is illegal, or that advertises payment to surrogates.
  • Medical advice for an individual's situation.

Advertising and partners

Advertising (including Google AdSense) and sponsorships fund the site. Advertisers and partners never review, approve or edit editorial content, and sponsored items are labelled. Rankings or recommendations are never sold.

Corrections

If we get something wrong we fix it quickly and, for material changes, note the date of the update. Report an error through the contact form.

Use of AI tools

We may use software, including AI tools, to help research, draft and check content. Every published page is reviewed by a person against the cited sources before publication.
