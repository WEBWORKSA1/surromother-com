About us

Honest information for the two people at the heart of every surrogacy

Most surrogacy websites are run by businesses that earn money when you sign with them. That's not wrong, but it makes it hard to get a straight answer on costs, risks and the law.

SurroMother exists to give intended parents and prospective surrogates that straight answer first. We publish plain-language guides, a law tracker covering 48 countries and every US state, and free tools that help you plan, budget and ask better questions.

What we are, and what we are not

  • We are an independent information and tools website, supported by advertising, clearly labelled sponsorships, professional listings and readers.
  • We are not a surrogacy agency, clinic, law firm, escrow company or insurer. We don't recruit or pay surrogates, arrange surrogacy, or hold anyone's money.
  • When you ask for help, a person reviews your request. With your consent, we can share it with licensed professionals who fit your situation and jurisdiction. You decide whether to work with anyone.

Our principles

  1. The surrogate's wellbeing matters as much as the parents'. We explain her rights, risks and protections in every relevant guide.
  2. The law comes first. We never promote paid surrogacy where it is illegal, and we flag what is lawful where you live.
  3. Sources on everything. Every country page and guide lists its sources and review date.
  4. No invented stories. We don't publish fake testimonials or unverifiable success rates.
  5. Advertisers don't edit us. See our editorial policy.

Who runs SurroMother

SurroMother.com is an independent web publication. Ownership and business enquiries are handled through web.works. Our content is written and maintained by an editorial team and reviewed against official sources; we are recruiting paid medical and legal reviewers (see careers).
