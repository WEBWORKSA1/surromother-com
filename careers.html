We're hiring · remote

Help families and surrogates get the facts

We're a small, independent team building the most trustworthy surrogacy resource on the internet. We pay fairly, credit our contributors and value lived experience.

Contract, remote

Medical reviewer (RN, NP or MD)

Review guides on IVF, pregnancy and the surrogate medical journey for accuracy and plain language. Reproductive or obstetric experience preferred.

Contract, remote

Family-law reviewer

Licensed attorney or solicitor to review country and state pages. US, Canadian, UK, Mexican and Colombian expertise especially welcome.

Freelance, per article

Health writer

Research and write sourced, plain-language guides. Experience with medical or legal topics required; samples needed.

Freelance

Translators (Spanish, French, Portuguese, Hindi, Mandarin)

Volunteer, honorarium

Community ambassadors

Former surrogates and parents through surrogacy who can answer questions in moderated sessions and review our content for lived-experience accuracy.

Commission-based

Partnerships lead

Freelance

Video editor and social producer

Turn guides into short explainer videos and carousels; motion-graphics skills a plus.

How we work

  • Remote-first, asynchronous, flexible hours.
  • Paid per project or monthly retainer; ambassadors receive an honorarium.
  • Every published contributor is credited by name (or pen name).
  • No commissions tied to sending people to any agency or clinic.
  • We welcome applicants from every country and background.
