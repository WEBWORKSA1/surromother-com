    Official rules (summary)

    1. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
    2. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18+. Employees of SurroMother and prize sponsors are not eligible.
    3. Entry period. Each contest runs from the first to the last day of the calendar month (UTC). One entry per person per month.
    4. Judging. Entries are judged by the SurroMother editorial team on honesty and helpfulness to others (40%), clarity (30%), relevance to the theme (20%) and originality (10%). This is a contest of skill; no element of chance determines the winners.
    5. Prizes. Three prizes as listed above, delivered as digital gift cards within 30 days of the announcement. Prizes are non-transferable and may be substituted with a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
    6. Content. Entries must be original, must not include another person's private medical information without consent, and must not advertise paid surrogacy, request or offer payment, or include contact details for arranging surrogacy. We may edit for length and privacy.
    7. Rights. You keep ownership of your work and grant SurroMother a non-exclusive licence to publish winning and shortlisted entries with credit.
    8. Privacy. We use your details only to run the contest. See the privacy policy.
    9. Questions go through the contact form.
