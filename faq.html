About SurroMother

Is SurroMother a surrogacy agency?

No. SurroMother is an independent information and tools website. We do not recruit surrogates for payment, arrange surrogacy, hold funds or provide medical or legal services. When you request a consultation we pass your enquiry, with your consent, to professionals you can then evaluate yourself.

How does SurroMother make money?

Is the information legal or medical advice?

No. It is general information reviewed against official sources. Surrogacy law changes often and depends on where every party lives, so always speak to a lawyer and a fertility doctor in the relevant jurisdictions.

Intended parents

How much does surrogacy cost?

In the United States most journeys cost roughly US$100,000-250,000 including surrogate compensation, agency, IVF, legal and insurance costs. Altruistic systems such as Canada and the UK cost less in fees but usually involve long waits. Use the cost calculator for an itemised estimate.

How long does a surrogacy journey take?

Typically 15-24 months from first consultation to bringing your baby home: 1-6 months to match, 2-3 months for screening and contracts, about a month to transfer, then pregnancy and post-birth paperwork. Altruistic countries can add a year or more of waiting.

Do we need embryos before we start?

No, but having tested, frozen embryos shortens the journey and makes success rates easier to predict. Many agencies and clinics require embryos before matching.

Can single people and same-sex couples use surrogacy?

In most US states, Canada, the UK and Australia, yes. Many other countries restrict access to married heterosexual couples. Check the law tracker for your destination and your home country.

Will my baby get citizenship and a passport?

It depends on your nationality, the birth country and how parentage is established. International journeys need advice from an immigration lawyer in your home country before you start. US executive orders issued in August 2026 add uncertainty for non-citizen parents using compensated surrogacy in the US.

What is the difference between an agency and an independent journey?

An agency recruits, screens and matches surrogates and coordinates the journey for a fee. An independent journey usually starts with a surrogate you already know and uses a clinic, lawyers and an escrow company directly. It costs less but puts more responsibility on you.

How do we protect our money?

Use a licensed, bonded escrow company that is independent from the agency, read the fee schedule line by line, and never pay large sums upfront to a business you cannot verify.

What questions should we ask an agency?

Ask about licensing, years operating, how surrogates are screened, average match time, rematch policy, who holds escrow, all fees and refund terms, and how they support surrogates. Our agency checklist lists 30 questions.

Surrogates

What are the usual requirements to become a gestational surrogate?

Most programmes look for people aged about 21-40 (some up to 45), who have carried at least one healthy pregnancy and are raising a child, have a BMI within the clinic's range, do not smoke or use drugs, have no serious pregnancy complications and live in a jurisdiction where surrogacy is lawful. Take the 2-minute pre-screen.

Will I be genetically related to the baby?

Not in gestational surrogacy. The embryo is created with the intended parents' or donors' eggs and sperm.

Can surrogates be paid?

It depends entirely on where you live. In most US states compensation is lawful. In Canada, the UK, Australia, India and many other countries only documented expenses may be reimbursed, and payment beyond that is illegal.

How long does it take to become a surrogate?

From application to embryo transfer usually takes 3-6 months: screening, matching, legal agreements and medication. Pregnancy and recovery follow.

Do I need my own lawyer?

Yes. Your lawyer should be independent from the intended parents' lawyer and paid for by them. They review the agreement with you before any medication starts.

Who makes medical decisions during the pregnancy?

You do. Agreements discuss expectations, but a surrogate keeps the right to make decisions about her own body and to receive appropriate medical care.

What medical risks are involved?

The same risks as any pregnancy, plus those of IVF medications and the possibility of multiples or a caesarean section. Screening, single-embryo transfer and good prenatal care reduce, but do not remove, risk. Discuss your history with your own doctor.

Can I be a surrogate if I had a caesarean?

Often yes. Many clinics accept up to two or three previous caesarean deliveries, depending on your history and the time since the last birth.

Does SurroMother pay surrogates?

No. SurroMother is not an agency and does not offer payment to anyone. If you complete the pre-screen we can share general next steps and, with your consent, connect you with licensed professionals where surrogacy is lawful.

Law

Where is compensated surrogacy legal?

In most US states, and in a few countries such as Georgia, Ukraine, Armenia and Kyrgyzstan, with residence, marital and nationality rules. Several former destinations closed to foreigners recently, including Greece (2025) and Kazakhstan (2026).

Where is surrogacy banned?

Among others: France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Switzerland, Austria, Finland, Norway, Slovakia and China. Italy also criminalises its citizens arranging surrogacy abroad.

What is a pre-birth order?

A court order, issued before the baby is born, that names the intended parents as the legal parents so they appear on the original birth certificate. Available in supportive US states.

What is a parental order?

In the UK, the court order that transfers legal parenthood from the surrogate to the intended parents after the birth.
