# SurroMother.com — Idea, Market & Competitor Research

Research date: 12 November 2026. Figures are taken from public pages visited on that date; they are not audited and should be re-checked before being quoted to partners.

---

## 1. The idea (decision)

**SurroMother.com = the independent surrogacy navigator.**
A neutral, global education + decision-tools hub for the two sides of every journey, with two high-intent lead funnels:

| Side | What they get free | What they hand over (lead) |
|---|---|---|
| Intended parents (IPs) | Cost calculator, country-by-country law tracker, US state guide, journey timeline, agency question checklist, 14 long-form guides, videos, news | "Get matched" consultation request: residence, destination, embryos, budget, timeline, services needed, contact → scored 0–100 |
| Prospective gestational surrogates (GCs) | Eligibility pre-screen (age, prior birth, BMI, nicotine, complications, C-sections, location), honest guides on medical process, compensation rules and risks | Pre-screened application with an eligibility verdict attached |
| Professionals (agencies, clinics, attorneys, insurers, lenders, counsellors) | — | Paid listings, sponsorships, advertising, partner enquiries |

### Why this idea beats the alternatives

1. **Highest-value leads in the vertical.** Agencies report paying up to **US$4,000 to acquire one intended parent** and **US$3,000–15,000+ per matched surrogate**; IP cost-per-lead runs **US$100–500**, surrogate inquiries **US$50–200** (Web Tonic benchmark compilation, 2026). Only **10–25 % of surrogate applicants qualify**, so a pre-screened surrogate application is the scarcest asset in the industry.
2. **High-CPC content for AdSense.** Fertility search ads average **US$9.84 CPC**, with surrogacy keywords **20–40 % higher** in competitive metros (Web Tonic, July 2026). Advertisers in this niche (clinics, agencies, lenders, insurers, egg banks) bid aggressively.
3. **Neutral position is open.** The 39 sites reviewed are either agencies (biased, single-country) or non-profits/advocacy (no tools, no matching). Nobody combines a live multi-country law tracker + cost calculator + two pre-screen funnels.
4. **The law is moving fast** (Spain 2025, Greece 2025, Slovakia 2025, Kazakhstan 2026, US executive orders Aug 2026, Thailand reform draft 2026, Colombia ban bill 2026). A tracker that is kept current earns links, return visits and authority.
5. **Market size.** Published estimates disagree wildly — from ~US$0.2 billion (IMARC) to US$23–28 billion (Market.us, SNS Insider, GMI) for 2025 — but every source shows double-digit growth and North America as the largest region. US journeys cost IPs **US$100,000–250,000+**.

### The flaw you must design around (non-negotiable)

- **Canada (where the owner lives):** the Assisted Human Reproduction Act s.6 makes it an offence to pay a surrogate, to **accept payment for arranging a surrogate's services, to offer to arrange them for payment, or to advertise such arranging**; penalties reach C$500,000 and/or 10 years. Selling surrogate leads to agencies for cash from Canada can be read as accepting consideration for arranging a surrogate's services.
- **India:** Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021 s.38 bans any advertisement "in any manner regarding commercial surrogacy", including online, up to 10 years.

**Therefore the build:**
- Positions SurroMother as an education + advertising platform, not an agency or intermediary. It never offers anyone payment for being a surrogate and never promises a match.
- Routes every form to the owner inbox only. What happens to a lead commercially is a business decision the owner makes **after advice from a Canadian AHR lawyer** (and, if pursued, through a non-Canadian entity contracting only with professionals licensed where compensated surrogacy is lawful).
- Detects the visitor's chosen country and shows jurisdiction-specific notices (Canada, UK, Australia, India and other altruistic-only or prohibited countries get "expenses-only / not permitted" messaging).
- Revenue that is clean everywhere: AdSense, sponsorship, directory/advertising listings for licensed professionals, supporter contributions.

### Revenue model (12-month targets, illustrative)

| Stream | Assumption | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense | 60,000 page views × RPM US$15–35 (health/fertility) | US$900–2,100 |
| Sponsorship & listings | 8–12 professionals × US$199–599 | US$1,600–7,200 |
| Lead partnerships (only after legal sign-off) | 80 IP consult requests × US$100–250 + 25 qualified GC applications × US$200–500 | US$13,000–32,500 |
| Supporters | 40 × US$10 avg | US$400 |

The lead line is where the money is; the law line is where the risk is. Do not switch on per-lead sales until the legal structure is in place.

### Patterns adopted
- Split audiences in the first screen (parents / surrogates / professionals) and repeat the split CTAs.
- Eligibility quiz with an instant verdict before asking for contact details.
- Cost transparency with itemised ranges and a "last updated" stamp.
- Country and state pages for long-tail SEO.
- Trust devices: medical/legal review notes, editorial policy, "we are not an agency" clarity, escrow and red-flag education.
- Non-profit style giving (one-time/monthly, where-the-money-goes), events/contests, membership-style newsletter segmentation.
- Advertising/partner page with packages (IVF Babble, Babygest model).

### Patterns rejected
- Unverifiable success-rate claims and invented testimonials (AdSense and consumer-protection risk).
- "Earn up to $X" surrogate pay headlines (illegal advertising in Canada and India; policy risk).
- Copying any agency's law map, wording or imagery.

## 3. Key facts used on the site (with sources)

- US journey cost: US$100k–200k+ (Circle Surrogacy, American Surrogacy, 2026); US$178k–282k at one premium agency (Elevate, Oct 2026); California often >US$200k.
- First-time US surrogate base compensation: roughly US$50k–75k in 2026 depending on state and agency (American Surrogacy: US$55k–65k+; several agencies US$70k–75k in premium states).
- Canada: AHRA s.6; reimbursement of receipted expenses only; penalties up to C$500,000 / 10 years (Health Canada).
- UK: surrogate is legal mother at birth; reasonable expenses only (typical £10k–15k per HFEA citing Surrogacy UK); parental order required (HFEA, GOV.UK).
- Greece: Law 5197/2025 Art. 46 — intended mother and surrogate must be legal residents; single men and male couples excluded.
- Spain: Instruction of 28 April 2025 — foreign surrogacy birth records no longer registered directly.
- Kazakhstan: amendment signed 26 July 2026 — surrogacy limited to legally married Kazakh citizens.
- Thailand: draft amendment in public consultation July 2026 to open altruistic surrogacy to foreign married couples incl. same-sex couples; commercial remains banned.
- Colombia: no statute; bill PL 215/2026 (Cámara) filed August 2026 proposes a ban.
- Ireland: AHR Act 2024 enacted; largely uncommenced; AHRRA established Oct 2025; s.232 commenced 20 April 2026.
- US: Trump v. Barbara (30 June 2026) upheld birthright citizenship; executive orders of 6 Aug 2026 target citizenship recognition in some commercial-surrogacy cases with non-citizen parents; challenged in court; guidance pending.
- Ad economics: Web Tonic 2026 benchmark pages (CPC, CPL, CPA by side of the market).
